# Custom Resume Builder

> **Build a tailored resume for every job application.**

Custom Resume Builder is an AI-powered resume generation tool designed primarily for **software, engineering, IT, and other technical roles**.

Instead of using the same resume for every application, provide your personal information and a job description, select your preferred AI model and output format, and generate a resume tailored to the requirements of that specific job.

## Live Demo

**[customresume.duckdns.org](https://customresume.duckdns.org)**

## Features

- **AI-Powered Resume Generation**
  - Generate job-specific resumes using an AI model.
  - Tailor your resume content based on the target job description.

- **Job-Specific Customization**
  - Create a different resume for each job application.
  - Focus your experience, skills, and projects around the requirements of the role.

- **Multiple Output Formats**
  - Export resumes as **PDF**
  - Export resumes as **DOCX**

- **ATS-Friendly**
  - Designed with Applicant Tracking Systems in mind.
  - Focuses on clean structure, relevant skills, and job-specific content.

- **Built for Technical Roles**
  - Software Engineering
  - Backend / Frontend Development
  - Full-Stack Development
  - DevOps & Cloud
  - Data & AI/ML
  - IT & Infrastructure
  - Other engineering and technical positions

- **Complete Resume Profile**
  - Personal information
  - Professional summary
  - Skills
  - Work experience
  - Education
  - Projects
  - GitHub
  - LinkedIn
  - Portfolio / Website

- **Local Personal Data**
  - Personal resume information is stored locally in your browser.
  - Your profile can be edited and reused when generating different resumes.

- **Multiple AI Providers**
  - Configure your preferred AI service.
  - Select the model used for resume generation.
  - Provide your own API credentials.

## Why Job-Specific Resumes?

A single generic resume doesn't always communicate the most relevant parts of your experience for every position.

Custom Resume Builder lets you maintain your complete professional profile while generating a version specifically tailored to the requirements of each job.

For example:

```text
Your Profile
     │
     ├── Backend Engineer Job
     │       └── Backend-focused Resume
     │
     ├── DevOps Engineer Job
     │       └── DevOps-focused Resume
     │
     ├── Full-Stack Engineer Job
     │       └── Full-Stack-focused Resume
     │
     └── AI/ML Engineer Job
             └── AI/ML-focused Resume
